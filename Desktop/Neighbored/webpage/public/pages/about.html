Frequently Asked Questions:

1.How much does it cost to download and use?

Neighbored LLC is free to use for users. Cities pay subscritption fees to be activated on the app.

2.Why does Shady wanna put LLC on everything

3.How do I create an event in my party

Events can be created by going to a city page and adding an event. Once the city admin has approved the event it will show in the feed.

4.Why cant I download Neighbored in the app store

Neighbored is a Progressive Web App. What does that mean for users? It means that the app takes up less space on users phone and runs faster while using.

5.Why does my app keep loading but nothing else happens

Neighbored uses your location to show you what is going on around you. If your browswer's location services are off the app will not load. Turn on location services for the app to load properly.
