About Us

Our Mission Statement is to connect rural communities through sharing community, business, and school events. Growing up in Coon Rapids, IA was special for us. We believe that rural communities like ours have a lot of events to offer people, but there is currently a lack of event awareness. To solve this issue, we have created Neighbored. Neighbored is a mobile app designed to bring rural communities together through sharing events on a common forum. This common forum allows locals and people in surrounding towns quick and easy access to the events that neighboring communities have to offer.
